# Spectra that would settle three H-0005 stars (request, 2026-10-28)

Each of these stars has a long, multi-instrument light curve and two or three live explanations that one low- or
moderate-resolution optical spectrum separates. All three are in VSX under types we think are wrong or incomplete.
Dossiers: `hunts/H-0005/`. Referee reports: `hunts/H-0005/reviews/`.

| priority | star | RA, Dec (J2000) | brightness now | when to observe | what decides it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gaia20fgk** (Gaia DR3 523457286761045120; VSX NL/VY:) | 01:08:33.49 +64:08:24.2 | g ≈ 17.0, r ≈ 16.2 (ATLAS o 16.3, Oct 2026) | **now**: circumpolar from the north, well placed in autumn; it was hidden for most of 1991-2015 and again 2020-2023, so the bright window may not last | Li I 6708 absorption + G-type metal lines + Hα / [O I] 6300 emission → young-star fader (referee's H1). Broad Balmer + He I / He II emission, weak photosphere → nova-like in low states (H2). Radial velocities every ~20 d test the 20-day modulation as a binary period |
| 2 | **Gaia20cdd** (Gaia DR3 1624711296862985472; VSX NL/VY:) | 16:20:53.76 +59:26:22.4 | G ≈ 16.1 out of dips (ATLAS o +0.1, Oct 2026) | low in the evening now; best Feb-Sep 2027. Ideally once out of a dip and once inside one | Hα in absorption, no Li, metallicity typical of the thick disk, RV consistent with its 80 km/s tangential motion → an **old dwarf with warm dust** (our reading; the nova-like type is already excluded by GALEX UV and Gaia Hα). A spectrum during a dip gives the reddening law and any gas absorption (Na D, K I) from the occulting material |
| 3 | **Gaia20fmb** (Gaia DR3 2900506558769385088; VSX VAR) | 05:42:32.12 -34:13:02.0 | ATLAS o ≈ 14.8-15.1 (still 0.4-0.7 mag below its 2019 level) | **now** (southern telescopes; rises in the evening); 21" from a G = 11.4 star, so a slit at the right angle | Li I 6708 and Hα / Ca II emission → a young (Peter Pan-type) M0 dwarf with a disk, 28° from the plane; no Li, no emission → an older dwarf with a cold debris disk being occulted, which would be stranger. RV gives membership in any young association |

Not requested: Gaia20cmg (VSX YSO:, quiescent at G ≈ 18.8; spectrum low value until it brightens again).
