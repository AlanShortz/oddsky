# OS-0013: SPHEREx check of the 2018 infrared excess (2026-10-30)

Independent checker. I made no edits outside this file and `spherex-2026-10-30/`.

**Verdict: the excess is still present in 2025-26.** At 4.4-5.0 µm, SPHEREx puts the star **+36 ± 3%** above
its pre-2018 photosphere, calibrated against the 27 field stars of C1. NEOWISE's post-2018 level predicts +41% at
that wavelength, and no excess would give 0%. None of the 27 field stars is higher than +8%. The excess is
present in all three SPHEREx seasons (2025 Aug-Sep, 2026 Feb-Apr, 2026 Aug-Oct), and its position matches the
K dwarf's to within 0.05". This is the second-instrument confirmation that the 28 Oct brief asked for. It
roughly reproduces the size of the excess (80-90% of the NEOWISE plateau level), not just its presence.

## Data and access route

- **IRSA SIA v2** (`https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/SIA?POS=circle+220.96627+-76.19612+0.002`). It returns 487
  SPHEREx Level 2 spectral images over MJD 60886.9-61299.4 (2025 Aug 8 to 2026 Oct 13), in two collections:
  - `spherex_qr2`: 304 images, processing versions v20-v24.
  - `spherex_qr3`: 183 images, version v27.
  - The images cover all six detectors (D1-D6, 0.75-5.0 µm).
- **Cutouts.** I downloaded 0.2° cutouts of every image through IBE (`<access_url>?center=ra,dec&size=0.2`). All
  487 arrived; 503 errors from IRSA cleared on retry.
- **IRSA's own tool not used.** IRSA's Spectrophotometry Tool, which does Tractor forced photometry, is part of
  the SPHEREx Data Explorer web app, and I found no API for it. I ran my own forced photometry instead.
- **Format change between releases.** QR3 replaced the QR2 per-exposure `PSF` cube (10× oversampled, 11×11
  zones) with an `EPSF` binary table (5× oversampled pixel-integrated ePSF, 21×21 zones). A future
  `oddsky.data.spherex` client must handle both formats.

## Method (`spherex-2026-10-30/scripts/`)

- **Forced PSF photometry.** For each image and star, I fit a 13×13-pixel stamp (6.15"/px) with the
  per-exposure PSF of the nearest zone plus a background plane.
  - Positions are fixed: Gaia DR3 propagated to the image epoch, otherwise CatWISE.
  - Neighbours brighter than the measured star's W1 + 3 mag get free fluxes. Fainter ones are subtracted at
    their catalogue-interpolated flux.
  - The wavelength at the star comes from the image's `WCS-WAVE` lookup table.
- **Measured stars.** The target and the 27 NEOWISE-flat field stars of C1 (pre-step W2 9.5-12.5, 80-300"
  away) are measured identically in all 487 images. The target is measured in 482 of them.
- **Photospheric reference.** For each star I interpolate (log-log) between 2MASS J, H, Ks and the 2014-17
  pre-step NEOWISE W1, W2 visit medians. For the target, that reference is its pre-2018 photosphere.
- **Calibration.** The field stars' median SPHEREx/reference ratio, per release and per window, is the
  calibration. It is 0.95-1.05 in every window, so the absolute scale agrees to within 5%.
- **Uncertainty.** The quoted error combines the field stars' star-to-star robust scatter (the systematic for a
  single star) with the target's own standard error.
- **"Expected" values** are what the NEOWISE post-step state gives at the wavelength measured: a 1045 K
  blackbody scaled to the W1/W2 excesses (11.188→11.020, 11.259→10.899).

  normalisation. Target relative to the field median:

  | Ratio | Target | Field robust sd | Expected if persisting |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | W2-like / K | +29.7% | 3.4% | +36.4% |
  | W2-like / W1-like | +20.4% | 2.3% | +20.9% |
  | W1-like / K | +7.6% | 2.3% | +12.8% |

- **By season, W2-like:**

  | Season | Images | Excess |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2025 Aug-Sep | 27 | +33.9% |
  | 2026 Feb-Apr | 21 | +33.6% |
  | 2026 Aug-Oct | 35 | +38.4% |

  W1-like gives +11.7%, +13.3% and +13.1% for the same seasons. The field calibration moves by less than 1%
  between seasons.
- **Robustness of the W2-like excess:**

  | Variant | Excess |
  |---|---|
  | Aperture, r = 2 px, annulus 4-7 px, fitted neighbours subtracted | +37.0 ± 4.0% |
  | 9×9 stamp | +34.7% |

  | Quadratic background | +34.0% |
  | Raw aperture, neighbours left in | +40.5 ± 7.1% |

  The raw aperture also reads +8-9% at J/H/K, which is the neighbours' light.
- **Centroid** (`phot/centroid.txt`). I fitted the target's position separately in the D3 (1.6-2.4 µm) and D6
  images, relative to 18 field stars.
  - The D6 minus D3 shift is +0.05" (RA), +0.03" (Dec) ± 0.03". The field stars' rms is 0.08" / 0.05".
  - If the ~27% of 4.6 µm light that is excess sat on the 7.9" neighbour, the shift would be about 2.1"; on the
    13.4" neighbour, about 3.6".
  - So the excess is co-located with the K dwarf to within about 0.5" at SPHEREx resolution.

| 16.3" | Gaia DR3 5792808351322471680 = AllWISE J144349.60-761159.9 | G 14.40, K 12.70, W2 12.35 | 26% (K), 37% (W2) | free flux |

- **13.4" and 16.3" neighbours.** Their fitted SPHEREx fluxes come out 8% and 6% *below* their AllWISE 2010
  values at 4.4-5.0 µm.
  - Even if all of that deficit had been misassigned to the target, it would account for at most 3.4% of the
    target's flux.
  - The excess would still be at least +32%.
- **HD 128641.** It lies 5.3 px away, at the edge of the PSF model's support (±5 px in QR2, ±3.3 px in QR3). Its
  wing at the target is therefore absorbed by the background terms. The quadratic-background variant changes the
  W2 result by 0.02.

## Can SPHEREx distinguish +39% from 0%?

**Yes, decisively.**

- **Against 0%.** +35.8 ± 3.1% is 11σ from zero, and no field star exceeds +7.6%.
- **Against the full NEOWISE level.** The difference is 1.6σ, so SPHEREx cannot tell the full NEOWISE level apart
  from an excess that is 10-20% weaker.
- **Possible extra systematic.** The target reads +5-6% at H and K, ranked 1 of 27, and the dust predicts only
  +1-3% there. If that is a normalisation error (2MASS 1999-2000 photometry, or crowding), the right comparison
  is the colour test: +29.7 ± 3.4% against +36.4% expected, still 9σ from zero.
- **Range.** Either way, SPHEREx sees 80-90% of the NEOWISE excess in all three IR windows (W1-like 0.80,
  D5 0.76, W2-like 0.88 of the expected value). That is within the NEOWISE plateau range: W2 10.86-10.94 means
  +34% to +44% at W2. The W2/W1 colour of the excess matches the 1045 K expectation (+20.4% vs +20.9%).
- **Fading.** I cannot tell whether the excess has faded slightly since 2024 or this is a calibration offset of
  about 5%.

## Caveats

- **Relative photometry only.** This is relative photometry against field stars, not a calibrated SPHEREx
  spectrum. The SPHEREx absolute calibration was not tested independently.
- **Calibration per release.** QR2 and QR3 are calibrated separately. I fitted each release's PSF in the way
  that minimised χ² on the field stars: QR2 integrated over pixels, QR3 point-sampled as an ePSF.
- **Unexplained near-IR offset.** The +5-6% H/K offset of the target is not explained. It does not affect the
  conclusion, but it widens the plausible range of the excess to about +30% to +37%.
- **Field stars are not K3V dwarfs.** Their colours span the range of field FGK stars. Colour terms between the
  broad WISE and narrow SPHEREx bands are absorbed only on average. At W2, the field scatter (2.9%) contains
  this effect.

## Not checked

- The onset: SPHEREx starts in 2025.
- A dust-temperature fit to the 3-5 µm SPHEREx spectrum. D5 (+22% against +29% expected) hints at a slightly
  different shape, but I did not fit it.
- Comparison with IRSA's Tractor-based Spectrophotometry Tool, which is web-only.
- Variability within a season.
- Spectral features.
- Whether the dust orbits the K dwarf or an unresolved companion: SPHEREx only places it within about 0.5".

## Reproduce

Run from `spherex-2026-10-30/`:

```
uv run python -I scripts/fetch_catalogs.py data
uv run python -I scripts/spherex_dl.py data/sia_spherex.csv <cutout_dir>
uv run python -I scripts/spherex_phot.py <cutout_dir> data phot/phot_base.csv
uv run python -I scripts/spherex_ana.py phot/phot_base.csv f_psf
```

- `spherex_dl.py` writes about 190 MB of cutouts. This run's are kept outside the repo, in
  `~/oddsky-runs/os0013-spherex-cutouts-2026-10-30/`.
- Variants use the environment variables `OS13_HALF=4`, `OS13_FREE_DMAG=6`, `OS13_BG=2` and `OS13_CENTROID=1`,
  followed by `scripts/centroid_ana.py`.
- Per-image photometry is in `phot/phot_base.csv`. Summaries are in `phot/*summary*.csv` and `phot/ana_*.txt`.
