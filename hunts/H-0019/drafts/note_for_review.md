the advisor,

Great catching up. Two things you said on the call stuck: that nobody uses Hubble's automatic multi-visit photometry, and that most AGB stars in other galaxies have two data points each, which isn't enough to say much about them. So I tried the obvious dumb thing: two points say little about one star, but what do they say about a whole population?

What I did. I took the Hubble Source Catalog and, for 13 galaxies within 4.5 Mpc, kept the red stars (those with a measured colour) up to 1 mag above the red-giant tip that have at least two clean ACS/WFC F814W visits, the first and last at least 100 days apart. That is 15,145 stars. Then I counted how many changed by more than 0.3 mag between those two visits, and subtracted the same count for stars just below the tip in the same images.

What came out:

- The excess is 3.4 ± 0.2% over all 13 galaxies, and 4.4 ± 0.3% in the five least crowded fields. Measured as nearly the same way as I could from real OGLE light curves, the LMC gives 3.7% and the SMC 2.4%. So it is a number in the range the LMC gives.
- As a check against known variables, 65 of 91 published Miras in M33 (Yuan et al. 2017) changed by more than 0.3 mag in a single pair of visits. For other stars of the same brightness it is 2.9%. (My first attempt at this check was wrong, a half-arcsecond offset between the catalogues, and was redone after a referee pass caught it.)
- In M33, 0 of 3,195 of the stars above the tip changed that much between visits less than 10 days apart, so short-term photometric scatter is not producing it there. Over all galaxies it is 33 of 9,125, nearly all in crowded discs.

What it is not: a result about metallicity. I preregistered a test of whether luminous galaxies score higher than faint ones, and it failed. Crowding in the aperture photometry, and where a fixed colour cut falls on giant branches of different age and metallicity, both move the number as much as galaxies differ. It is a method that works and a number in the LMC's range, nothing more yet. The three figures are attached. The full write-up is in the order you described, figures first, and I can send it.
