# H-0019 literature read (independent agent, 2026-11-09)

A separate agent was given the question and the method, not the result, and asked what is already published. Most entries
rest on abstracts; only Caldwell+1998 and the Roman white paper were read directly. "Found no paper" is a search result.

**Closest precedents**
- Caldwell, Armandroff, Da Costa & Seitzer 1998 (AJ, astro-ph/9710286): two WFPC2 visits of the M81 dwarfs BK5N (109 d apart)
  and F8D1 (56 d), |dm| > 4 sigma. Variable fraction above the tip: 5/14 and 39/158, as lower limits. Errors from
  visit-to-visit repeatability. The two-visit Hubble idea is theirs.
- Boyer+2015 (DUSTiNGS II, arXiv:1412.0695): two Spitzer epochs about 180 d apart in 50 dwarfs; 526 dusty variable AGB stars;
  33-61% of x-AGB stars variable in six galaxies; a simulation on OGLE Cloud stars recovers about 62%. No clear metallicity trend.
- Goldman+2019 (DUSTiNGS V, ApJ 877, 49): periods for 88 sources in seven galaxies; no discernible pulsation difference from
  the Magellanic Clouds down to [Fe/H] = -1.85.
- Conroy+2018 (ApJ 864, 111): M51, 34 HST epochs plus archival pairs; pairwise dm > 0.3 mag for decade-scale variability;
  noise from artificial stars, not from constant stars.
- Bonanos+2019 (A&A 630, A92), the Hubble Catalog of Variables: at least 5 detections in one instrument and filter. Sources
  with 2-4 visits are excluded. AGB stars are not discussed as a population.
- Moretti+2018 (MNRAS, arXiv:1803.09782): 38 new variables, nearly all long-period, in three M31 fields of the HSC.

**Fraction against metallicity**
- Schultheis, Glass & Cioni 2004 (A&A, astro-ph/0408126): Bulge, LMC, SMC. "The proportion of variable stars decreases at
  lower metallicities." Qualitative; no numbers found.
- Soszynski+2009, 2011 (OGLE-III): Miras are 1.8% of catalogued long-period variables in both Clouds. The denominator is
  detected variables, not stars above the tip.
- INT Monitoring Survey (Saremi+2020, 2021), Whitelock/Menzies/Feast, Lebzelter+2023: counts per galaxy; no tabulated
  fraction against metallicity found.
- Huang+2018, 2020, 2024 (Miras in NGC 4258, NGC 1559, M101): assume the LMC O-rich period-luminosity relation; no fraction modelled.

**Not found**
A uniform, quantitative large-amplitude fraction above the tip across many galaxies; any use of HSC sources below the
five-visit cut; an OGLE-based forward model as the reference; a forecast for few-epoch Roman imaging (Kraemer+2023 white
paper says even one epoch helps locate AGB stars).

**Caution raised**
Stars just below the tip are not strictly constant (small-amplitude red variables), so the RGB control needs the same
treatment in the reference as in the data. It has it: the reference subtracts the same box.
