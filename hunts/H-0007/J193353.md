## Evidence

- **Observed (NEOWISE, 2020 Apr 9-10, MJD 58948.9-58949.9):** 12 frames from 11 scans are ×1.98 brighter in both bands (ΔW1 = ΔW2 = −0.74)
  against a 19-visit baseline (W1 13.06, robust scatter 0.02). No artifact flags, normal χ², centroid on the star. 2,021 stars within 20'
  in the same frames and the adjacent visits are unchanged (W1 12.6-13.6: +0.010, MAD 0.025); the target is the only one beyond −0.3 mag.
  The W1 slope across the visit is not significant (+0.083 ± 0.022 mag/d, within the ±0.09 mag/d of the star's own baseline visits).
  One moon-masked frame on Apr 14 (Moon 6° away) gives W1 ×1.74 and W2 ×2.00 ± 0.30; it is not used as evidence.
- **Observed (ZTF, IRSA DR, catflags = 0, corrected with 12 colour-matched stars on field/CCD/quadrant 285/15/2):** the star is 0.17 mag
  bright on 2020 Jun 18 and 0.08-0.10 on Jul 14-21. The change is achromatic (Δg = Δr to 0.01), so the extra light has the star's own
  colour, g−r = 0.76 ± 0.15. The star is the only one of 35 g and 49 r stars in its quadrant beyond −0.08. In the same Jun-Jul window of
  2018, 2019 and 2021-2025 it sits at 0.00 ± 0.01 at the same airmass. ZTF has no data between 2019 Nov 5 and 2020 Jun 18.
- **Computed (referee):** a point-source, point-lens fit to NEOWISE and corrected ZTF gives t0 = 2020 Apr 10 (Δχ² < 4: Apr 2-17), tE = 56 d
  (48-62 d), u0 = 0.575, A_max ≈ 1.95, χ² 229/423, with no blending needed. The NEOWISE visit is at the peak. The model also matches the
  2020 Sep NEOWISE visit (×1.02) and ZTF 2019 Nov and 2020 Oct (−0.01 to −0.02).
- **Computed (referee):** the optical depth on this sightline is about 9×10⁻⁸, eight times J040025's. Among H-0007's 64.5 M faint stars a
  few A ≳ 2 events should have been caught on a NEOWISE visit, so one candidate is expected, not lucky.

## Novelty (searched 2026-10-29)

Not in VSX (120"), the ASAS-SN, ZTF, ATLAS or Gaia DR3 variable catalogues, TNS (60"), SIMBAD (CfA mirror, 120"), the Gaia Science Alerts,
KMTNet 2020 (nearest event 17° away), or a web search. The OGLE EWS index has no 2020 season; MOA 2020 was unchecked.
Gaia transited it on 2020 Feb 28, Mar 12 and Apr 17 (predicted ΔG −0.35, −0.49, −0.71) without an alert.

## Alternatives

A flare, nova, dwarf nova or companion outburst would change the colour (the extra light has the giant's own colour from g to W2) and would
not decay smoothly over three months. A blend is ruled out: no source within 9.7", and the centroid is fixed. Artifacts are ruled out:
two independent instruments, each the only star in its field that changed. Microlensing fits both data sets with no free blending.
